Atlântico Energia

Overview

Atlântico Energia is a well-established Brazilian company in the energy infrastructure sector. The company manages dozens of simultaneous projects involving complex technical analyses, structural reports, field reports, and regulatory documentation.

Category
Role

Project introduction

This case documents the solution of the Munnu project, an innovative digital platform created to solve a critical bottleneck at Atlântico Energia.

Scenario

Documentation is essential to ensure compliance with regulations, effective communication between teams, and informed decision-making. However, the company faced significant challenges in document management and organization.

Problems found

The team of specialists spent hours daily just reading and filtering files (reports, minutes, field reports, technical proposals), often identifying critical risks only when problems had already occurred, impacting project deadlines and costs.

Challenge

Optimize the reading and screening of operational documents to reduce risks and time, as well as help analysts identify risks and inconsistencies before they become problems. Also, use AI for reading and screening operational documents.

Design Process

Following the Double Diamond approach, we divided the project into 3 major deliveries

Problem context

Stage where we conducted research and market data analysis. Understanding the usage profile, client pain points, user pain points, and generating personas.

Value proposition

Strategic vision of how we use UX to solve user pain points and be profitable for the company. As deliverables for this stage, we created the value proposition canvas and the UX canvas, always seeking balance between user and business.

Solution context

Moment when we developed the product resolution proposal.
