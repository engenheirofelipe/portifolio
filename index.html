Who am I ?

I am a 22-year-old in the fifth semester of Software Engineering at FIAP in São Paulo. I am motivated to deliver technology solutions for problems present in society. My vocation is in Project Management and UX/UI Design as I have a high degree of empathy with users and am always seeking to study and understand human behavior so that I can deliver solutions to problems.

I have a very creative mind and I don't just live on technology. I am also a multi-instrumentalist musician, composer, and singer. I produce my own songs and release my music to the world. I have a musical project called Saving Lipe that is available on all digital platforms.
